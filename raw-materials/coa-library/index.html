Open or download each report directly. No inquiry or registration is required. These are LotusBio logo-watermarked distribution copies; the original report content, test values, dates and signatures remain in place.

Match the report to the material

Each document applies to the sample and specification described in that report, not every batch or current stock. Check identity, sequence or molecular form, dates and test scope against the material proposed for your order. A raw-material COA must not be substituted for a finished-vial report.

The LotusBio watermark identifies the distributed copy. It does not change the original issuing information or independently authenticate the results. These files are not presented as accredited independent laboratory reports. Research use only; retain the restrictions printed in each PDF.

Names on the source files and inside reports can differ. In particular, the Thymulin report is listed as Thymulin, and the TB500 document identifies Thymosin beta4; confirm exact identity before comparing materials.
