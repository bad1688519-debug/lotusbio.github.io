What size should a vial label be?

Confirm the vial body diameter and the usable straight-sided label height before choosing a label size. A nominal vial capacity or a product's mg specification does not define its outside dimensions. Send a dimensioned drawing or measurements of the intended vial for review.

Specify whether you want a partial-wrap or wraparound label. Confirm the proposed width, height, corner shape, gap or overlap, and application position against that vial before approving a dieline. A dieline is the template that identifies the label's finished cut shape.

What information goes on a research peptide label?

Prepare a field list before arranging the design. Keep fixed brand artwork separate from product-specific and batch-specific text, and nominate who approves each field.

Field groupWhat to confirm
Brand and product identityBrand name, full product name, catalog code and stated vial specification.
Variable identifiersBatch or lot reference and any date fields supported by the agreed product records.
Research-use wordingAccurate intended-use text and any destination-specific wording reviewed by the buyer.
Barcode or QR code, if requestedThe final data or destination URL, allocated space and a readability check on the proposed printed label.

Use the same product identity and specification on the vial, its printed packaging box and order record. If a QR code links to a COA, verify the exact report and applicable sample or batch; a link alone does not establish that a report covers the order.

Which label materials and artwork files should I request?

Material & adhesive

Describe the vial surface, application conditions and expected storage or handling. Ask which label stock and adhesive can be reviewed for those conditions.

Print files

Provide the editable artwork if available and a review PDF. Obtain the confirmed dieline, bleed, colour and export requirements before preparing the final production file.

Physical fit

Discuss whether a printed or applied sample is needed. Confirm its availability, cost and timing separately; a digital proof reviews layout, not adhesion on a vial.

Do not assume a label is waterproof, solvent-resistant or suitable for refrigerated or frozen storage from its appearance. Agree on the required performance and how it will be checked. See the vial label artwork approval checklist for file preparation and proof review.

How does label approval work?

  1. Define the vial and order. Send the selected product codes, vial measurements, destination and quantities by design.
  2. Confirm the scope. Review label dimensions, material, printing, application responsibility and any matching boxes with our team.
  3. Check the proof. Review product text and variable fields at actual label size. Request a physical sample review where appropriate.
  4. Approve the exact files. Record the artwork filename, version and approval date. Confirm any subsequent change before production.

Custom vial label questions

Can I request labels and matching boxes together?

Yes. Include both in the same private-label inquiry, with the vial count per box and the product identities for each design. Label and box specifications are confirmed separately before the project is agreed.

Can I order labels without peptide supply?

Tell us explicitly if you need labels only. This page describes packaging coordination for research supply projects; acceptance of a standalone label order must be confirmed by our team.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Request a quotation for your quantity per design. There is no single published minimum covering all label dimensions, materials, product mixes and packaging projects.

Can I use one label for different products or strengths?

A shared layout can be proposed, but each product name, catalog code, specification and variable field must be checked for the corresponding order line. Approve the relevant versions separately.

Can international buyers submit artwork?

Include the destination country, required language and label wording in the inquiry. Our team reviews project feasibility and shipping arrangements; the buyer should arrange appropriate review of local requirements.
