OEM / PRIVATE LABEL

Peptide OEM & private-label supply.

LotusBio coordinates private-label research peptide supply for laboratory and wholesale projects. Start with the product and vial specification, then define labels, box presentation and outer packaging. Available options, artwork and batch documents are reviewed for your proposed order.

Discuss your project ↗

What can a private-label project include?

Bring product specifications, label application, box presentation and neutral outer-packing requirements into one supply request. The agreed scope depends on the selected material, quantities and destination; each packaging element is confirmed before production.

Vial formats and product mix

Select the catalog code and vial specification for each product, with a separate quantity for each line. A mixed-product inquiry should show the required box count per specification. Keep a bulk raw-material request separate from the finished-vial packaging brief.

Labels and artwork

Submit your existing artwork, or outline the information and visual layout required for review. Identify the brand, product name, specification, label dimensions if known and any variable batch fields. Confirm the final wording, dimensions and artwork version before label application.

Boxes, inserts and outer packaging

Describe the vial count, box presentation, insert cards and neutral outer-packing requirements. Include any barcode or shipping-label requirements in the brief so they can be checked alongside the destination details.

How does an OEM inquiry move forward?

  1. Define the supply request. List products, catalog codes, quantities, destination and the intended research procurement use.
  2. Review the proposed scope. Confirm available material and packaging options, applicable documents, project minimum quantity and preparation timing in the quotation.
  3. Approve the artwork. Review the agreed label and box files, variable information and final version before production is confirmed.
  4. Confirm the order details. Match the product specification, document references, packaging instructions and delivery requirements to the agreed quotation.

Which specifications should I include?

Use the product pages to select the listed formats rather than requesting a product by name alone:

  • Retatrutide vial specifications
  • Tirzepatide vial specifications
  • Semaglutide vial specifications
  • Cagrilintide vial specifications
  • GHK-Cu vial specifications
  • NAD+ catalog formats

Listed catalog formats do not automatically establish which custom packaging options are available. Include the selected code and your requirements in the wholesale supply inquiry for project review.

Is there one minimum quantity or lead time?

There is no single figure stated here for every OEM project. Product mix, packaging components, artwork readiness and destination affect the scope. Request a written quotation that states the minimum quantity for each relevant line, preparation timing and shipping arrangements before committing to the project.

How are COAs handled with private-label packaging?

Browse the public COA library and third-party testing information before your inquiry. A new label or box does not change what an existing report proves. Keep report identifiers and the connection to the proposed material and batch clear when reviewing your branded presentation.

Research-use supply, clearly presented.

Products and packaging are intended for laboratory and research procurement, not human or veterinary use. Artwork should accurately identify the supplied material and its intended use. A packaging service does not establish a regulatory approval, certification or test result for the finished presentation.
