Planning a Private Label Peptide Packaging Project

LotusBio Editorial · 10 June 2026

Organize your label artwork, batch information and packaging approval process.

Define the packaging brief

List the vial format, order quantity, label dimensions, carton requirements and artwork files needed for the project. Confirm which items the supplier provides and which your team must prepare.

Reserve space for variable information

Plan where product identifiers and batch information will appear. Agree on the review process for variable text so that the final packaging can be matched to the relevant order records.

Approve a proof before production

Review spelling, readability, dimensions and barcode requirements where applicable. Request a production proof or sample and record approval of the exact version that will be used.

Agree on change control

Confirm how artwork revisions, packaging substitutions and order changes will be handled. For a project quotation, send your packaging brief to the LotusBio team.
