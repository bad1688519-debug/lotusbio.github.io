WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy & analytics choices

Updated 26 November 2026

Inquiry information

When you send an inquiry, LotusBio uses the information you provide to review your requirements and respond. Our forms are delivered through FormSubmit to our business email. Email and WhatsApp links open the service you choose; those providers apply their own privacy terms. Do not include sensitive personal or medical information in your inquiry.

Optional Google Analytics

We load Google Analytics only after you choose “Allow analytics.” It helps us understand page visits, referral sources and actions such as opening a contact link or viewing a report. A lead event is sent only after the inquiry service confirms acceptance for delivery. It does not confirm a sale or a sales response.

We do not send form entries, names, email addresses, phone numbers or inquiry messages to Analytics. Page URLs sent by our measurement code exclude query strings and fragments. We do not enable Google Signals or advertising personalization. Google processes device and browser information and may use cookies to distinguish visits. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choice

Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the site or submitting an inquiry. Use “Analytics preferences” in the footer to change your choice. The preference is stored in this browser for up to 180 days; Analytics cookies are configured for up to 180 days. Revoking consent disables subsequent measurement and clears the Analytics cookies accessible to this website. Previously collected data is not automatically erased.

Essential local storage

The site stores your quote list and certain interface choices in your browser to support the requested functions. These are separate from optional Analytics. Clearing browser storage removes locally saved choices and your quote list.

Contact

For questions about your inquiry information or privacy choices, email info@lotusbio.cn. Please identify the relevant inquiry without sending unnecessary sensitive information.
