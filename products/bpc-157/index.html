Which BPC-157 vial format should I request?

Choose BC5 for 5 mg vials or BC10 for 10 mg vials. Each catalog box contains 10 vials of the selected specification. These are standalone BPC-157 entries; a blend containing BPC-157 is a separate product request and should use its own catalog code.

Ten vials per box is the catalog packaging unit, not a confirmed minimum order. State the box count for each code; the quotation confirms minimum quantities and availability for the requested specification and destination.

Quantity example: BC5 lists 5 mg per vial and 10 vials per box. An inquiry for 2 boxes means 20 vials at 5 mg each. These are catalog label quantities, not batch assay results.

BPC-157 molecular structure & identity

BPC-157 is a 15-residue peptide. The reference sequence is Gly–Glu–Pro–Pro–Pro–Gly–Lys–Pro–Ala–Asp–Asp–Ala–Gly–Leu–Val (GEPPPGKPADDAGLV). The diagram below shows the corresponding parent peptide.

Sequence and terminal groups

Sequence order, stereochemistry and terminal groups are part of the molecular identity. A salt, counterion or other modified form should be named explicitly in the specification rather than inferred from the BPC-157 name.

BPC-157 batch comparison

For repeat orders, compare the identity assignment and analytical method with the earlier batch. Read the chromatogram alongside any identity result; a purity percentage by itself does not establish the amino-acid sequence or the quantity of BPC-157 supplied.

Reference information describes the database compound, not a batch test result. Confirm the supplied form and identity against the specification and available batch documents. Molecular weight is reproduced at the precision reported by PubChem.
