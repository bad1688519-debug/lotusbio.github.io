MOTS-c wholesale ordering

Which MOTS-c vial format should I request?

Use MS10 for 10 mg vials, MS15 for 15 mg, MS20 for 20 mg or MS40 for 40 mg. Keep the box count beside each code when comparing quotes. These are separate pack specifications; the amount printed for one vial does not describe the quantity of vials in the order.

Ten vials per box is the catalog packaging unit, not a confirmed minimum order. State the box count for each code; the quotation confirms minimum quantities and availability for the requested specification and destination.

Quantity example: MS40 lists 40 mg per vial and 10 vials per box. An inquiry for 2 boxes means 20 vials at 40 mg each. These are catalog label quantities, not batch assay results.

PRODUCT IDENTITY & REFERENCE

MOTS-c: mitochondrial-derived peptide reference

MOTS-c was described as a 16-amino-acid peptide encoded within the mitochondrial 12S rRNA region. The reference image depicts the database peptide, rather than mitochondrial DNA or a protein carrying a MOTS-c tag.

Reference peptide and modified constructs

A tagged construct, a sequence mutant and an unmodified peptide are different research materials. Specify the complete sequence and terminal form when comparing MOTS-c listings; the biological name alone does not describe an experimental construct.

What to confirm for this catalog item

The MS10, MS15, MS20 and MS40 entries distinguish pack quantities. Confirm identity and handling documentation for the actual material; a database illustration is not a stability study for a supplied vial.

Images and numerical properties describe the cited database forms, not batch test results. Confirm the actual supplied form against the specification. Molecular weights retain the precision reported by PubChem.
