KLOW80 purchasing questions

Does 80 mg mean 80 mg of every component?

No. The catalog lists 80 mg as the combined amount per vial. Request the intended amount and material form of each component in the proposed formulation. The measured values in the sample report above do not define the nominal formula for every order.

What should I include in a wholesale inquiry?

State KLOW80, the number of 10-vial boxes, destination country and postal code, and required timing. Ask the quote to confirm component specifications, the supply batch, available tests and any label or box requirements. Availability, minimum quantity and delivery arrangements are confirmed for the project.

Can I purchase vials for my own laboratory testing?

Ask about KLOW sample vials before placing a larger order. Sample availability, vial quantity, cost and shipping require consultation. Record the batch being evaluated so the test results can be matched to that material.
