MOLECULAR REFERENCE

NAD+ molecular structure & identity

NAD is a dinucleotide coenzyme rather than a peptide. Its structure contains nicotinamide and adenine nucleotide portions joined through a diphosphate linkage. NAD+ denotes the oxidized member of the NAD+/NADH pair.

Reference formula
C21H27N7O14P2
Reference molecular weight
663.4 g/mol
Database identifier
PubChem CID 5892

Reference charge state

The PubChem record used here is the NAD zwitterion (nadide, CID 5892). The formula and diagram describe that particular representation, not every protonation state, salt or hydrate sold under the NAD+ name. NADH is a distinct reduced form and should not be used as a substitute structure.

NAD+ material documentation

Confirm the oxidized form and the stated salt or hydrate in the batch specification. Check the basis used for the reported content, including whether water or counterions are accounted for. Storage and handling conditions should follow the documentation for the actual supplied material.

Reference information describes the database compound, not a batch test result. Confirm the supplied form and identity against the specification and available batch documents. Molecular weight is reproduced at the precision reported by PubChem.
