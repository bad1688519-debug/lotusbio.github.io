MOLECULAR IDENTITY

TB-500: confirm the peptide identity

The TB-500 name should be checked against an explicit sequence and terminal modifications. A published analytical study identified an N-terminally acetylated seven-residue fragment, Ac-LKKTETQ, in a product described as TB-500. That fragment is not the same molecule as full-length thymosin beta-4.

What to match in the specification

Ask for the complete amino-acid sequence, terminal groups, expected molecular mass and any salt or counterion designation for the proposed material. BT5 and BT10 are the pack codes in this catalog; they do not, on their own, establish whether the material corresponds to a fragment or the full-length peptide.

Why no single structure is assigned here

A molecular diagram for this catalog item must match its confirmed material specification. Until that identity is established, assigning a fragment or full-length reference image would imply a chemical identity that the pack listing alone does not verify.
