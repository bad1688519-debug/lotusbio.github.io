KPV purchasing details

Select the catalog format

KP5 and KP10 are the listed KPV formats. Use the catalog code as well as the product name to keep the 5 mg and 10 mg options separate in repeat orders.

Quantity example: KP10 lists 10 mg per vial and 10 vials per box. An inquiry for 2 boxes means 20 vials at 10 mg each. These are catalog label quantities, not batch assay results.

Vials or bulk raw material?

The quote selector on this page uses boxes of finished vials. For bulk raw-material requirements, specify the required total mass, material specification and container requirements separately; the vial pack does not establish a raw-material minimum order quantity.

Documents and quotation scope

Check the sequence and terminal form against the material specification. If measured vial content is needed, request that result explicitly rather than relying on purity alone. Use the COA review checklist to record the product, batch and test scope.

Send the selected code, box count, destination country and requested delivery date. Ask the quote to confirm availability, minimum quantity, preparation time, shipping service and cost. If custom labels or boxes are needed, include them in the packaging inquiry. Delivery timing and batch documents are confirmed for the proposed order. MOLECULAR REFERENCE

KPV molecular structure & identity

KPV denotes the three amino acids lysine, proline and valine in that order. The reference shown here is L-lysyl-L-prolyl-L-valine, also listed as MSH (11–13) in PubChem.

Reference formula
C16H30N4O4
Reference molecular weight
342.43 g/mol
Database identifier
PubChem CID 125672

Which KPV structure is shown?

This reference has a free carboxyl terminus. A C-terminal amide, an acetylated derivative or a different stereoisomer is a different chemical structure. Confirm terminal groups and counterion form in the material specification before using this diagram for comparison.

KPV specification review

KP5 and KP10 identify the pack options shown above; the pack code alone does not specify terminal chemistry. Ask for the chemical identity and available batch-linked analytical documentation as well as the requested vial quantity.

Reference information describes the database compound, not a batch test result. Confirm the supplied form and identity against the specification and available batch documents. Molecular weight is reproduced at the precision reported by PubChem.
