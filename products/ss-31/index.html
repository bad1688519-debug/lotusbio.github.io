What are the SS-31 pack sizes and minimum order?

The SS-31 entries are 2S10 and 2S50. Use these codes and the vial specification together, especially when discussing a document that names Elamipretide.

Ten vials per box is the catalog packaging unit, not a confirmed minimum order. Minimum quantities and availability are confirmed for your requested specification and destination.

Quantity example: 2S10 lists 10 mg per vial and 10 vials per box. An inquiry for 2 boxes means 20 vials at 10 mg each. These are catalog label quantities, not batch assay results.
