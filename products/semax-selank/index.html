Semax + Selank blend purchasing questions

Is this a 20 mg vial or two separate 10 mg vials?

XSL20 is the combined entry: Semax 10 mg plus Selank 10 mg in one vial. Each box contains 10 such vials. For separate single-component packs, use the Semax catalog or Selank catalog.

What do I send for a wholesale quote?

Include XSL20, the 10 mg + 10 mg composition, number of boxes, receiving country and postal code, and required timing. Add any requested analytical tests, batch documents or private-label packaging. Minimum quantity, availability and shipping are confirmed with the quotation.

Can I purchase sample vials for independent testing?

Ask about XSL20 sample vials. Availability, vial quantity, cost and shipping require consultation. Confirm that the testing laboratory can evaluate both components in the blend and keep the sample batch reference with the results.
