COMPONENT IDENTITY

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin: two components

This catalog entry names a combination of two peptides. A blend does not have one covalently connected molecular structure or one molecular formula representing both ingredients as a single compound. Each component needs its own identity reference.

Confirm the CJC-1295 form

CJC-1295 terminology requires care: published analytical work distinguishes CJC-1295 and CJC-1295 with drug affinity complex. The CJC component should be specified by its sequence and modifications before a structure diagram is assigned.

Confirm component quantities

The CP10 and CP20 catalog options list 10 mg and 20 mg pack specifications. Those labels alone do not establish the amount of each component or their ratio. The quotation and batch specification should state CJC-1295 content and Ipamorelin content separately, together with the form of each peptide.

Review analytical coverage

When requesting documentation, ask which component identities and quantities the method addresses. A single overall purity figure should not be read as a separate assay result for each peptide.
