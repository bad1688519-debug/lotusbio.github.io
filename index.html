WHOLESALE FAQ

Answers before your first inquiry.

Are LotusBio products independently tested?

Selected products have independent third-party reports available through the report library. Documentation is sample- or batch-specific, so ask the wholesale team to confirm the report connected to your requested product.

Can you support OEM and private-label orders?

Yes. Label application, packaging coordination and neutral wholesale formats can be discussed after product, quantity and destination are confirmed.

How do I receive wholesale pricing?

Send the product, specification, box quantity and destination by email or WhatsApp. Current stock, pricing, lead time and delivery options are then confirmed.

What is the standard pack format?

The standard catalog format is 10 vials per box. Other packaging requirements can be reviewed for confirmed wholesale projects.
