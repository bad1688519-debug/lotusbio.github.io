How to use this library

From a study headline to a useful question

01 · Identify the material

Check the molecule, formulation and model before comparing research. Similar names do not always identify the same material.

02 · Read the actual endpoint

Look for the population, comparator, duration and measured outcome. Keep cell findings separate from human clinical outcomes.

03 · Verify the batch

A publication explains research. A lot-matched report documents a sample. Use both for their intended purpose.
