CHINA WHOLESALE SOURCING

Peptide Wholesale Sourcing from China

For laboratories, distributors and resellers comparing China-based research peptide suppliers, the useful starting point is a documented specification, a defined purchase format and a clear quotation workflow.

Define the exact supply format first

Separate finished lyophilized vial inquiries from bulk peptide raw-material inquiries. The product name alone is not enough to prepare an accurate B2B quotation.

  • Product and catalog specification
  • Vial strength or raw-material quantity
  • Box or bulk quantity
  • Destination country
  • Required documentation and packaging

Review supplier documentation before scaling

Ask which COA, analytical report or batch record applies to the material proposed in the quotation. Purity, content, identity and endotoxin are separate analytical scopes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Use a test order to verify the procurement workflow

A first order can be used to verify communication, packaging, document matching and shipment handling before a larger repeat order. Keep the quotation, batch reference and received documentation together for comparison.

Finished vials, raw materials and OEM are different projects

LotusBio separates finished-vial wholesale supply, bulk raw materials and private-label packaging so the requested format is clear before pricing and production coordination.

Prepare a complete wholesale request

Send the product, exact specification, estimated quantity, destination and documentation requirements in one message. This reduces repeated clarification and makes supplier comparison easier.
