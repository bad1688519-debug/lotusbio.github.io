How do US buyers check a research peptide COA?

Start in the public report library, then match the product, material format, report number and sample or batch identifier to the proposed quotation. Keep the report's test methods and units with the recorded results.

  • Finished-vial report: check the sample identifier and any measured content for that vial.
  • Raw-material COA: check the material identity, batch and listed tests for the bulk material.
  • Blend report: check which components were identified and measured; a combined purity figure does not establish a separate result for every component.

A catalog code identifies a specification, not a unique batch. Use the COA verification checklist to ask which document applies to the material being quoted.

Can I purchase sample vials for independent testing?

Ask about purchasing sample vials and state the product, specification, desired vial quantity, planned tests and US destination. Availability, sample quantity, cost and shipment arrangements require consultation. Keep the supplied sample's batch identifier with the laboratory results.

Private-label peptide packaging for US projects

List the product codes and quantities, then specify whether you need custom vial labels, printed vial boxes or both. Include the vial's outside diameter, usable label height, vial count per box and a review PDF of the artwork. Mark the units on every drawing.

For longer copy, request a multi-page booklet label review. Dimensions and page count are confirmed for each project. Identify fixed brand artwork and the fields that change with the product or batch; review the label and box together before approval.

What should a US wholesale quotation include?

  • Product and specification; vial boxes or total raw-material mass with units.
  • Receiving organization, US state and ZIP code.
  • Proposed batch and the documents available for it.
  • Minimum quantity and price for each order line, plus any sample or packaging charges.
  • Dispatch location, preparation time, shipping service and transit estimate shown separately.
  • Packaging, receiving documents, delivery charges and any costs or responsibilities excluded from the quote.

Minimum quantities, availability and shipping arrangements are confirmed for the proposed order. If you need a required receipt date, state it in the inquiry and ask the team to confirm feasibility.
