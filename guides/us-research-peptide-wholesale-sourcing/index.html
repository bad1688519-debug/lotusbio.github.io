Research Peptide Wholesale Sourcing for US Buyers

LotusBio is based in Guangdong, China and supports research-use wholesale inquiries from international buyers, including the United States. This guide focuses on the information a US buyer should prepare when comparing a China-based peptide supplier.

Use an exact product and specification

Send the product name, vial strength or raw-material format, required quantity and destination. Separate finished-vial orders from bulk raw-material requests so the quotation and documentation match the intended supply format.

Review batch documentation before ordering

Ask which COA or analytical report applies to the proposed material. Match the sample or batch identifier, test scope and report number rather than relying on a general catalogue purity claim.

Prepare the US destination and shipment information

Provide the destination state and receiving details needed for the quotation. International availability, carrier options and documentation can vary, and buyers should independently confirm the requirements that apply to their organization and intended research use.

Read the wholesale shipping guide →

Compare supplier workflow, not only unit price

  • Specification and packaging clarity
  • Batch-document availability
  • Communication and response process
  • Shipping preparation and tracking expectations
  • Repeat-order and private-label requirements

Use the wholesale supplier checklist to compare these items consistently.

Build one complete B2B request

Include product, specification, quantity, destination, requested documentation and packaging requirements in one inquiry. For bulk material, use the raw-material supply section; for finished research vials, use the product catalogue.
