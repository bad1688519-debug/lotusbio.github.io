Custom peptide packaging boxes

Plan printed vial boxes around the contents they need to hold. Review dimensions, artwork and packaging quantities with LotusBio as a standalone inquiry or part of a private-label research supply project.

How do I choose a custom peptide packaging box? Begin with the actual vial dimensions and number of vials per box. Then confirm the internal fit, any insert, outer dimensions and print template. LotusBio coordinates custom printed boxes to agreed buyer specifications; material, finish and quantities are confirmed before production.

Single-vial boxes, multi-vial boxes or shipping cartons?

Single-vial box

For a brief covering one vial per printed box. Identify the vial dimensions, opening style and any holder or insert to review.

Multi-vial presentation

State the exact vial count and layout. Include each vial size and whether the contents are one specification or a mixed set.

Outer shipping carton

Describe how the inner boxes should be grouped for dispatch. Discuss protection, carton markings and destination requirements separately.

These are formats to discuss in your project brief. Availability and construction are confirmed for the proposed order; a printed presentation box does not by itself establish transport protection or temperature control.

Which box dimensions should I provide?

Give dimensions in millimetres and state whether they describe the inside usable space or the finished outside box. Include a drawing that identifies length, width and height. Vial count alone does not determine the box size because the vial geometry and insert arrangement also matter.

How should box artwork match the vial labels?

Use the confirmed box dieline before placing artwork. Identify the front, back, side panels and opening orientation, and keep essential product text clear of the agreed cut, fold and glue areas. Confirm the template and print requirements with the production team.

Match the product name, catalog code, vial specification and vial count to the approved order. Reserve any required lot or other variable fields and identify who supplies their final values. Review box and label proofs side by side to catch inconsistent wording.

Prepare files using the label and box artwork checklist. Submit your brand files and review PDF with the brief; final production file requirements are confirmed for the project.

What should I approve before box production?

  1. Dimensions and construction: review the selected box, vial layout and insert; clarify inside versus outside measurements.
  2. Digital artwork proof: check text, panel orientation, variable fields, code destinations and the named file version.
  3. Physical sample, if agreed: discuss a sample to review fit, closure and printed presentation. Confirm sample availability, cost and timing in the quotation.
  4. Written production specification: record the accepted material, finish, quantities, artwork revision and packing instructions. Reconfirm changes before production.

A packaging sample and a research vial purchased for independent testing are separate requests. If you need both, identify each in your inquiry so availability and arrangements can be discussed.

Custom packaging box questions

Do you have a standard box size for every peptide vial?

No universal fit is stated here. Confirm the actual vial dimensions, count and insert before selecting a box. The 25 × 25 × 60 mm example must not be assumed to fit every vial.

Can I request a 10-vial box with my own branding?

Include a 10-vial layout in your inquiry and provide the vial dimensions and product mix. Our team will review the proposed box, insert, artwork and order quantity before confirming availability.

What are the minimum quantity and lead time?

They are quoted for the proposed dimensions, construction, print designs and quantities. Include your desired delivery date and destination; preparation and shipping timing require separate confirmation.

Can I request custom boxes only?

State clearly that your inquiry is for packaging only. The accepted scope, quantities and supply arrangements must be confirmed before an order is agreed.

What do international buyers need to include?

Send the destination country, required wording or languages, carton-marking instructions and delivery preferences. Confirm shipping terms and any applicable destination requirements for the project.
