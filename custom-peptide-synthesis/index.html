What to include in a custom synthesis request

  • Sequence or a non-confidential description of the project.
  • Total target quantity in mg, g or kg, and any preferred container format.
  • Target purity and any separate peptide-content or identity-testing requirements.
  • Requested terminal groups, modifications or other project-specific requirements.
  • Required batch documentation, destination and preferred timing.

Requested length, scale, purity, modifications and dates are project inputs, not statements of guaranteed production capability. Ask our team to confirm which requirements can be supported before placing an order.

Custom peptide synthesis questions

Is custom synthesis the same as private-label packaging?

No. Custom synthesis starts with sequence-defined project requirements. Private-label packaging concerns product presentation, labels and outer packaging. Tell us if your project needs both services so each scope can be reviewed.

Can you confirm a sequence, modification or quantity before I order?

Submit your project requirements for review. Our team confirms feasibility and proposed specifications individually; this page does not set universal sequence-length, scale or modification limits.

How are purity and documentation agreed?

State the target purity and any identity, peptide-content or other analytical requirements. Ask which documentation is available for the proposed project, and confirm the agreed specification and batch reference before ordering.

When will the project be delivered?

Preparation, synthesis and shipping requirements are reviewed with the quotation. A requested date or a historical small-parcel shipping example is not a delivery commitment for a custom synthesis project.
