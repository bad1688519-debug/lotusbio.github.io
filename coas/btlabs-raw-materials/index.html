Sample weight: the laboratory record takes precedence.

The submitted vials were labelled 100 mg. LotusBio’s submission note states that 50 mg of raw material was placed in each vial for testing. The actual submitted sample mass is determined by the laboratory’s recorded weighing result, which takes precedence over the supplier’s fill statement. The 50 mg statement is not a laboratory-verified measurement or an amendment to the signed reports.

The original reports use a 100 mg content/potency basis and list a potency specification of 90–110% of 100 mg. Their measured masses, HPLC purity and potency results are reproduced separately below. The supplier’s fill explanation does not change the laboratory results or establish that the stated potency specification was met.

What these reports cover

These reports apply only to the identified raw-material samples as received. They do not establish the contents of finished commercial vials or the quality of another lot.

The listed test scope includes appearance, mass, FTIR identification and composition, HPLC purity and potency, and peptide-to-excipients ratio. The reports explicitly do not provide information about particulate matter, microbial contamination or endotoxins. HPLC purity and the amount measured in a sample are different results.

All PDFs retain the original laboratory branding, signatures and report numbers. Use the complete document, including its sample description and chromatograms, when evaluating a quotation.
