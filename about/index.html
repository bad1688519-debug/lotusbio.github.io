ABOUT LOTUSBIO

Peptide development, production and supply in one clear workflow.

LotusBio (莲花生物) is a peptide-focused biotechnology and international supply company in Guangdong, China, serving research, analytical, pharmaceutical-intermediate and cosmetic formulation projects.

COMPANY INTRODUCTION

Focused on peptide materials and project-based solutions.

LotusBio supports peptide product development, production coordination and international B2B supply. Our work covers high-purity peptide raw materials, finished lyophilized vial formats, active cosmetic peptide ingredients and custom/OEM project support.

For research and pharmaceutical-intermediate projects, we help buyers align product identity, specification, requested quantity, current documentation, packing and destination requirements. For cosmetic formulation projects, we support active peptide ingredient inquiries for anti-aging, repair, firming and related applications.

From the first technical question to repeat wholesale fulfillment, LotusBio keeps the supply process practical, documented and responsive.
